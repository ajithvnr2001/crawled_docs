Model ID: vertexai/gemini-3.1-flash-lite Provider: gemini Product: router, cloud, workflows Input: text, image, audio, video Output: text License: closed Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite is a text generation model from Google Gemini. It takes text, image, audio and video as input and produces text. It has no open weights. It runs on Google Gemini’s servers, so you use it through Comfy Router and Partner Nodes in ComfyUI and Comfy Cloud.

## Pricing

Comfy Router charges the model maker’s list price, billed from your workspace credits (211 credits = $1 USD). Current per-model prices are on [router.comfy.org](https://router.comfy.org).
