# Fix a failed Comfy Cloud payment

Copy pageCopy page

If a Comfy Cloud payment fails, update your payment method from Plan & Credits or the Payment failed banner to avoid a paused subscription.

Copy pageCopy page

If a Comfy Cloud payment fails, the workspace shows a **Payment failed** banner. Pay the unpaid invoice or update your payment method so the subscription does not pause.

## 

​

What happens when a Comfy Cloud payment fails

  1. Comfy Cloud shows **Payment failed** with the amount due. Pay the invoice or update payment to avoid a pause.
  2. If the charge still cannot be collected, the subscription can pause. The banner then says **Subscription paused**. Update payment to resume.
  3. Workspace members who cannot manage billing should ask the workspace owner to restore the subscription.



## 

​

How to update payment after a failure

  1. Visit [cloud.comfy.org](https://cloud.comfy.org/?utm_source=docs) and log in.
  2. Click **Pay invoice** on the banner to pay the unpaid invoice, or **Update payment** to change the card. You can also open the profile menu, click **Manage plan** , then in **Plan & Credits** click **Billing & invoices**.
  3. Update the payment method in Stripe and save.

See [Edit Comfy Cloud payment information](https://docs.comfy.org/support/payment/editing-payment-information).

## 

​

Common reasons a payment fails

### 

​

Insufficient funds

  * Your card does not have enough available balance or credit
  * **Solution** : Add funds or use a different payment method



### 

​

Expired card

  * Your credit or debit card has passed its expiration date
  * **Solution** : Update your payment information with a valid card



### 

​

Incorrect information

  * Card number, CVV, or billing address is entered incorrectly
  * **Solution** : Verify and correct your payment details



### 

​

Card declined by issuer

  * Your bank or card issuer has declined the transaction
  * **Solution** : Contact your bank to authorize the payment



### 

​

International restrictions

  * Your card does not support international transactions
  * **Solution** : Enable international payments or use a different card



### 

​

Security holds

  * Your bank has flagged the transaction as suspicious
  * **Solution** : Contact your bank to verify and approve the charge



## 

​

Preventing future payment failures

  * Keep your payment information up to date
  * Ensure your card has sufficient funds before billing dates
  * Update your card details before expiration
  * Ask your bank to allow charges from Comfy Organization Inc



## 

​

Need assistance?

If you continue to experience payment issues after trying these solutions, contact our [support team](https://support.comfy.org/) with:

  * Your account email
  * The error message you received
  * The payment method you’re attempting to use
  * Any relevant transaction IDs
