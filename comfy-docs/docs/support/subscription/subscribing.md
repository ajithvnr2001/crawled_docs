## 

​

How to subscribe to Comfy Cloud

You can browse Comfy Cloud without a subscription. You need a paid plan to run workflows. The **Choose a Plan** dialog has two scopes:

  * **For Personal** : individual plans (such as Standard, Creator, and Pro) with a fixed monthly credit amount.
  * **For Teams** : a workspace team plan. You set the monthly credit amount with a slider. Larger credit amounts can include a larger discount.

Yearly billing usually costs less per month than monthly.

  1. Visit [cloud.comfy.org](https://cloud.comfy.org/?utm_source=docs) and log in.
  2. Open the profile menu and click **Plans & pricing**, or click **Subscribe**.
  3. In **Choose a Plan** , choose **For Personal** or **For Teams**.



  * For Personal

  * For Teams




Personal plans are for individual use. Pick a named plan with a fixed monthly credit amount.

  1. Select a billing cycle: **Yearly** or **Monthly**.
  2. Select your plan and click **Subscribe**.
  3. Review **Confirm your payment** , add a payment method if asked, then click **Subscribe to** the plan name.



Team plans apply to your workspace. You choose how many monthly credits the workspace gets.

  1. Select a billing cycle: **Yearly** or **Monthly**.
  2. Drag the credit slider to set your monthly credit amount.
  3. Click **Subscribe to Team Yearly** or **Subscribe to Team Monthly**.
  4. Review **Confirm your payment** , add a payment method if asked, then click **Subscribe to** the plan name.



Plan prices, credit amounts, and discount rates can change. Use the amounts shown in **Choose a Plan** or on [comfy.org/cloud/pricing](https://www.comfy.org/cloud/pricing).

## 

​

Applying a promotion code

If checkout shows a **Promo code** field, enter your code and apply it before you pay. A valid code updates the total. Promotion codes can expire or have usage limits.

## 

​

Subscription renewal

Your subscription renews automatically on the same day and time as when you first subscribed. Monthly plans renew each month, and annual plans renew each year. Annual plans receive their full year of credits up front. All Comfy Cloud subscription plans automatically renew unless you set your plan to cancel.
