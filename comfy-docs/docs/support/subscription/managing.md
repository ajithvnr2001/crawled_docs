# Manage Comfy Cloud billing and invoices

Copy pageCopy page

Manage your Comfy Cloud plan, payment methods, and invoices. Change plans in Comfy Cloud; payment methods and invoices are in the Stripe billing portal.

Copy pageCopy page

## 

​

Accessing Comfy Cloud subscription management

  1. Visit [cloud.comfy.org](https://cloud.comfy.org/?utm_source=docs) and log in.
  2. Open the profile menu and click **Manage plan**.
  3. In **Plan & Credits**, click **Billing & invoices**.

This opens the Stripe billing portal for payment methods and invoices. To change your plan, use **Change plan** in Comfy Cloud instead. See [Change your Comfy Cloud plan](https://docs.comfy.org/support/subscription/changing-plan).

These steps apply to your **Personal** workspace. A **Team** workspace has its own subscription, and only the workspace owner can manage it from the workspace settings panel. See [Workspaces](https://docs.comfy.org/cloud/workspace).
