## **Hunyuan 3D: Minutes-Level 3D Creation in ComfyUI**

Generative AI has reshaped 3D content creation. Traditional pipelines—modeling, texturing, lighting, rendering—often take days or weeks. With **Hunyuan 3D** in ComfyUI, you can generate production-ready 3D assets in minutes by simply entering text prompts, images, or sketches, with far lower barriers to entry.

## **What You Can Create**

  * **Text-to-3D** : Enter a text description such as “Mechanical dolphin with gears, steampunk” and generate a 3D model in one click. The model accurately follows style, shape, and material details for consistent results.

  * **Image-to-3D** : Upload one or more images to generate high-quality 3D models. Support for 2–4 multi-view images improves geometry and material fidelity.

  * Advanced features coming soon: **Texture Editing** , **Semantic UV Unwrapping** and **3D Part Decomposition.**

## **Use Cases**

  * **Game Development Ideation** : Fast generation of characters, props, and environments with optimized topology for rigging and real-time rendering

  * **Product & E-commerce**: Turn product photos into interactive 3D models for online display

  * **Industrial Design** : Sketch-to-3D prototyping and 3D-print-ready part decomposition

  * **VFX & Animation**: High-resolution assets with professional UVs and PBR materials




## **Getting Started**

To use Hunyuan 3D in ComfyUI:

  1. **Update ComfyUI** : Ensure you have the latest version of ComfyUI or go to [Comfy Cloud](https://links.comfy.org/4dyliB6)

  2. **Access Workflows** : Template Library in the side bar → **3D** → **Hunyuan 3D**

  3. **Select Workflow** : Search and choose a workflow template about Hunyuan 3D

  4. **Run** : Input text, images, or sketches and run the workflow

[Run or download Hunyuan 3D 3.0 Workflow](https://cloud.comfy.org/?template=api_hunyuan3d_image_to_model&utm_source=blog)
