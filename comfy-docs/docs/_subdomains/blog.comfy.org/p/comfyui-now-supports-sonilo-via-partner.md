We’re excited to announce that [Sonilo](https://sonilo.com/) is now available in ComfyUI via Partner Nodes. Sonilo is an AI-powered video-to-music service that generates soundtracks synchronized to your footage—analyzing visuals, pacing, and emotional cues to produce music locked to your timeline, frame by frame.

You can now drive music generation directly from your ComfyUI node graphs, alongside your existing workflows.

[Try on Comfy Cloud](https://links.comfy.org/4cG2wHe)

## **Sonilo Highlights:**

  * **Length, Pace & Emotion Alignment** — Music is generated to match the exact duration, pacing, and emotional arc of your video, delivering soundtracks that feel intentionally composed for the content.

  * **Instant Generation** — Generate full-length, high-quality music in seconds, enabling faster iteration and a seamless creative workflow.

  * **Commercial-Ready Usage** — All generated music includes commercial use rights, making it ready for use across professional, brand, and production environments without additional licensing complexity.




## Who is Sonilo for?

[Sonilo](https://sonilo.com/) is built for creative professionals who need music that works as hard as the rest of their production. Whether you’re turning around dozens of assets a week or crafting a single high-stakes deliverable, Sonilo removes the bottleneck of sourcing and timing music manually — so you can stay in your creative flow from first cut to final export.

**Use cases:**

  * **Advertising & creative studios** — core product videos, brand spots, and social ads with music that matches the pacing of each cut, without waiting on a composer or digging through stock libraries.

  * **Video production & post houses** — automate soundtrack generation across large volumes of footage, keeping timelines tight and revision cycles short.

  * **VFX & motion graphics artists** — generate music that evolve with your visuals, frame by frame, for title sequences, reels, and motion pieces.

  * **Content creators & agencies** — produce platform-ready videos with synchronized music across multiple formats and aspect ratios without re-editing audio for every version.

  * **Developers & pipeline engineers** — integrate music generation directly into automated workflows via ComfyUI, removing manual handoffs between creative and technical teams.
