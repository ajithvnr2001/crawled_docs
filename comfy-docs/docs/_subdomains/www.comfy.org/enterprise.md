## Enterprise, answered.

### What is included in Comfy Enterprise?

Comfy Enterprise can combine ComfyUI Managed Builds, dedicated production capacity and support, commercial model licensing, and Forward Deployed Creatives. Teams that only need shared Cloud usage can start with the self-serve Team plan.

### Where can our Comfy Workflows run?

Comfy Workflows can run locally, on infrastructure you control, or through the Developer Platform. ComfyUI Managed Builds are designed for approved environments on your machines and infrastructure.

### How are Builds different from ComfyUI Managed Builds?

Builds are the self-serve way to package ComfyUI, custom nodes, models, and dependencies into a reproducible environment. ComfyUI Managed Builds add team distribution, governance, controlled rollout, enterprise terms, and support across the environments your organization runs.

### Can we use models commercially?

On Cloud, every model is licensed for commercial use. On local, licenses vary by provider and commercial terms are available on request. Comfy is also an official reseller for MiniMax commercial licenses.

### How does Comfy support security review?

SSO and a DPA are available. Comfy does not train on customer data, and our SOC 2 Type II audit is complete. Review current controls in the [Comfy Trust Center](https://app.vanta.com/comfy.org/trust/o6nu46b16iu3e7fhc41hnz).

### What usage and audit information is available?

Track usage by model and partner on the Developer Platform. Authentication, build-assignment, release, retention, and export requirements are scoped explicitly during enterprise security review.

### Is there a self-serve Team plan?

Yes. Team is a self-serve Comfy Cloud plan with member invites, concurrent workflow runs, a shared credit pool with centralized billing, and role-based permissions. Shared workflows, assets, and projects are coming soon.

### What production capacity and support are available?

Enterprise agreements can include dedicated GPU capacity, priority queueing, guided onboarding, direct access to Comfy engineers, and uptime and response commitments scoped to your requirements.
