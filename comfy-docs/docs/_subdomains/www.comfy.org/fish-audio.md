## Q&A

### What is Fish Audio Drama 3?

Fish Audio's newest model, and the one it calls its most emotive and controllable yet. You write a multi-character scene and set the direction, and Drama 3 performs it as a single conversation. It runs in the Fish Audio Text to Speech node, in Comfy Cloud and in local ComfyUI 0.39.3 or newer.

### How do I try Drama 3 in Comfy Cloud?

Open [Comfy Cloud](https://cloud.comfy.org) and search the Template Library for Fish Audio Drama 3, or add the Fish Audio Text to Speech node from partner nodes and set model to drama-3. Add your voices, write the dialogue, and run it.

### What is the launch offer?

Drama 3 is 50% off for your first 30 days in Comfy Cloud. Open [Comfy Cloud](https://cloud.comfy.org) to start.

### How does a scene fit into a workflow?

Drama 3 runs in the Fish Audio Text to Speech node, so it fits into a Comfy workflow like any other node. Build the scene and its audio in one graph. Change a line, run again, and everything downstream picks up the new take.

### Is Drama 3 open weights?

No. Drama 3 runs through Fish Audio's API, so there is nothing to download and no GPU to provision.

### What do the other Fish Audio nodes do?

Text to Speech also runs S2.1 Pro and S1, which take emotion cues in the text. Speech to Text transcribes audio with automatic language detection, and Instant Voice Clone makes a private voice from 1 to 20 recordings. Voice Selector is where you pick a voice from the Fish Audio library, and all four are in the [Fish Audio docs](https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/partner-nodes/fishaudio/fish-audio).

### Can I clone a voice with the Fish Audio nodes?

Yes, with the Fish Audio Instant Voice Clone node: connect 1 to 20 recordings, under 270 seconds in total and ideally 10 to 30 seconds each, and the cloned voice feeds straight into the Fish Audio Text to Speech node with no training wait. Voice cloning is free. Clone only voices you have permission to use.

### How are Text to Speech and Speech to Text priced?

Per use, through your ComfyUI account. Text to speech is metered by text length and speech to text by the minute. Current rates are in the [Fish Audio docs](https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/partner-nodes/fishaudio/fish-audio).
