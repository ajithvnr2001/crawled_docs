##  Q&A 

How do people usually use it?![](https://media.comfy.org/website/comfy-agent/plus.svg)![](https://media.comfy.org/website/comfy-agent/minus.svg)

Key capabilities: \- Builds, edits, reads, runs, and debugs workflows, running a task end to end \- Builds in real time while you keep editing the graph \- Curated knowledge for model recommendations and parameter tips \- References assets and nodes, dragged straight from the asset library \- Visual understanding of assets anywhere in Comfy \- Creates, saves, and deletes skills \- Up to 5 agent chats in parallel, with full chat history Coming soon: \- Local support, including creating custom nodes and model/node downloads \- GPU detection and workflow recommendations \- LLM model selection 

What model powers it?![](https://media.comfy.org/website/comfy-agent/plus.svg)![](https://media.comfy.org/website/comfy-agent/minus.svg)

Frontier models by Anthropic. We always provide the strongest we can offer for the job. Choices for switching LLMs is on the way. What they lack alone, the agent adds: full context of your workflow, your errors, and Comfy itself. 

Do you plan to support local LLMs?![](https://media.comfy.org/website/comfy-agent/plus.svg)![](https://media.comfy.org/website/comfy-agent/minus.svg)

Yes. We have local LLM support in the roadmap. ComfyUI can do the memory management for both the image / video models and the LLMs at the same time. 

Does it work with my existing workflows?![](https://media.comfy.org/website/comfy-agent/plus.svg)![](https://media.comfy.org/website/comfy-agent/minus.svg)

Yes. Open any workflow and the agent reads the whole graph. Describe what you want to change, or point at an error, and keep editing together. You can also reference another workflow in the chat. 

Will it run generations without asking?![](https://media.comfy.org/website/comfy-agent/plus.svg)![](https://media.comfy.org/website/comfy-agent/minus.svg)

You decide. By default the agent will ask your confirmation to run any generations. In auto mode it runs and iterates on its own until the result is ready. 

What does it cost?![](https://media.comfy.org/website/comfy-agent/plus.svg)![](https://media.comfy.org/website/comfy-agent/minus.svg)

Today, we provide some free tokens for new users to try the chat. Normally, Comfy Agent consumes the Comfy Credits for LLM tokens. Your existing credits can apply to it already. See the pricing page for plans and usage details.[See pricing](https://www.comfy.org/pricing/)

Can it run locally?![](https://media.comfy.org/website/comfy-agent/plus.svg)![](https://media.comfy.org/website/comfy-agent/minus.svg)

Local support is coming soon. The same agent will run in your local ComfyUI, working on workflows based on your GPU, your models, and your custom nodes. It will walk you through setup, pick models that fit your hardware, and build the tools you need. 

Does the agent replace the canvas?![](https://media.comfy.org/website/comfy-agent/plus.svg)![](https://media.comfy.org/website/comfy-agent/minus.svg)

No. The canvas stays at the center. The agent builds with you on it, every control stays where it is, and you keep the editable workflow behind every result. 

How is this different from asking ChatGPT?![](https://media.comfy.org/website/comfy-agent/plus.svg)![](https://media.comfy.org/website/comfy-agent/minus.svg)

ChatGPT only gives you instructions to follow. Comfy Agent works directly on your canvas. It sees your workflow and your errors, answers your questions, and also builds and runs the graph, and reviews the output. It doesn’t just see the entire setup, but also execute tasks for you.
