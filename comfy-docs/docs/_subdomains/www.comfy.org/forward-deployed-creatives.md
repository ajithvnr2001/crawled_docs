## How it works

01

### Validate

We prove the outcome is achievable on your own material.

02

### Build

Inside your Comfy Enterprise environment.

03

### Enable

We train your team to run, modify, and extend it themselves.

04

### Own

What we build is yours, and you can change it without us.

The direct line stays open. We're here when your team needs us.

  * Use-case validation and a proof-of-concept build
  * A production-grade workflow, built and installed
  * Enablement until your team is self-sufficient
  * White-glove access under your enterprise agreement
  * Reusable creative assets your team keeps

## Q&A

### Can this be a consulting or professional-services contract standalone?

No. It is an add-on to your Comfy enterprise agreement. We bring deep Comfy expertise to your use case, as an optional add on to your enterprise contract.

### Is it included in every enterprise contract?

No. It is available to any enterprise customer as an add-on, not bundled automatically into every contract.

### Will someone be embedded full-time with our team?

Forward Deployed Creatives work with you on defined milestones and deliverables, with optional in-person time as well. We will customize this based on what your team needs.

### What happens after the initial build?

You own the workflow and your team is trained to change it. We provide ongoing support and stay available under the enterprise agreement for the next thing, plus optional workshops and one-on-one training.

### Who are the people we would actually work with?

Production-credible creatives from feature film, commercial, and experiential backgrounds. Short intro bios/videos on the solution page let you see who you are getting before you sign.
