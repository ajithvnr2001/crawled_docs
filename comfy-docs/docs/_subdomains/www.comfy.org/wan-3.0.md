## Q&A

### What is Wan 3.0?

Alibaba's latest video model. Give it a prompt, up to 20 reference assets, or even a document and it generates up to 30 seconds with native audio.

### What's new in Wan 3.0 vs Wan 2.7?

Native 30-second clips, up to 20 reference inputs, and 480p generations for quick and cost effective generations.

### How do I run Wan 3.0 in ComfyUI?

This model ships with three workflows available in the template library — [reference to video](https://cloud.comfy.org/?template=api_wan3_0_r2v), [image to video](https://cloud.comfy.org/?template=api_wan3_0_i2v) and [text to video](https://cloud.comfy.org/?template=api_wan3_0_t2v).

### How do I prompt Wan 3.0?

Start simple: subject, scene, motion — one sentence is enough for a full clip. For more control, layer in camera movement, lighting, style, and sound, or address your uploads directly with @ syntax: "@Image1 walks through the door and says the line from @Audio1." The longer and more precise the prompt, the closer the output tracks your intent — it accepts up to 20,000 characters.

### Can Wan 3.0 edit video I already have?

Yes. Describe the change — remove an object, relight the scene, swap a subject — and everything else in the frame stays put. It can also extend existing footage.

### How long can clips be?

Anywhere from 2 to 30 seconds, generated in one continuous take — no stitching. Audio is generated natively, or switch it off.
