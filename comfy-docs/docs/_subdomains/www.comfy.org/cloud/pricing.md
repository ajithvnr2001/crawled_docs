## What's included in the Comfy plan

Machine Setup 

Comfy Cloud runs on Blackwell RTX 6000 Pro – 96GB VRAM

Time limit per job 

Each workflow run has a maximum duration of 30 minutes. On the Pro plan, the time limit is increased to 1 hour. Jobs exceeding that limit are automatically cancelled to ensure fair usage and system stability.

Usage 

You're only charged for **active GPU** time while a workflow is running. Idle time (e.g. time spent building workflows) does not consume GPU hours.

Credit balance 

All plans will include a monthly pool of credits that are spent on active workflow runtime and [Partner Nodes](https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/partner-nodes/overview) like Nano Banana Pro.

Add more credits anytime 

Purchase additional credits at any time. Unused top-ups roll over to the next month automatically for up to 1 year.

Pre-installed models 

Access a library of 900+ pre-installed models.

Custom nodes support 

Comfy Cloud currently supports a variety of the most-used custom nodes from the ComfyUI community. We're expanding support regularly based on demand and compatibility.

Partner Nodes 

Run **proprietary models** through Comfy's [Partner Nodes](https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/partner-nodes/overview), such as Nano Banana. The amount of credits each node uses depends on the model and parameters you set in the node, but these credits are the same ones that your monthly subscription comes with. These credits can also be used across **Comfy Cloud and local ComfyUI**. Read more about Partner nodes [here](https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/partner-nodes/overview).

Job queue 

Queue up to 100 workflows at once.

Custom LoRA importing 

For those on the Creator, Pro, or Team plans, you can bring in your own models & LoRAs from CivitAI or Huggingface to perfect your own style.

Parallel job execution 

Run multiple workflows in parallel to speed up your pipeline.

##  FAQs 

How does Comfy Cloud pricing actually work? 

Every plan includes a pool of **credits** — refilled each month on monthly plans, granted for the whole year up front on annual plans. Credits are spent on two things: **active GPU time** while a workflow is running, and **Partner Nodes** (proprietary models like Nano Banana Pro). You're never charged for idle time. Building or editing a workflow costs nothing. You only spend while a job is actually running.

What's a credit worth? How far does it go? 

Credits map to GPU runtime, so mileage depends on the workflow. As a reference point, a five-second video* uses roughly **11 credits** , so Standard covers a few hundred per month, Creator about double that, and Pro enough for over a thousand.

*Based on 5s videos using the Wan 2.2 Image-to-Video template at default settings (81 frames, 18fps, 640×640, 4-step sampler). Heavier models, higher resolutions, and the inclusion of Partner nodes use more.

What happens when I run out of credits? 

You can buy **top-up credits** at any time without changing plans. Plan credits are spent first; top-ups are only drawn down once your plan allowance is used up. Top-up credits stay valid for **1 year** from purchase.

Do unused credits roll over? 

Plan credits reset at the end of each billing cycle and don't roll over — monthly on monthly plans, and once a year on annual plans, which receive their whole year of credits up front. **Top-up credits do persist.** They're valid for a year and aren't affected by your plan's reset. Credits work on Comfy Cloud, and on Comfy Desktop **only when calling Partner Nodes.** Comfy Desktop itself is free.

What's the difference between Standard, Creator, and Pro? 

**Standard -** 30-min max runtime per workflow, 1 concurrent workflow via API. For individuals building workflows.

**Creator -** Everything in Standard plus the ability to **import your own models** (from CivitAI or Hugging Face) and run up to 3 workflows concurrently via API.

**Pro -** Everything in Creator plus **longer runtime (up to 1 hour)** per workflow and up to 5 concurrent workflows via API. For teams running Comfy in production.

How does the Team Plan work? 

The Team Plan puts your whole team on **one shared credit pool.** Every member draws from the same balance, so you're not juggling separate subscriptions. Key things to know:

**One pool, shared.** Everyone generates against the same credit balance.

**Invite by email.** Add teammates, and resend or revoke access any time.

**Owners manage billing.** Assign owners who handle payment and buy top-ups for the team.

**Upgrade in place.** Move an existing workspace to a team and your workflows, models, and assets stay attached.

Choose your monthly credit commitment that fits your team. [ Get started today. ](https://cloud.comfy.org/?pricing=team)

Can I control how my team spends credits? 

Today, owners control the shared pool and top-ups. We're actively building finer-grained controls: **spending limits** at the user, project, and workspace level, **per-project budgets and chargebacks** , **auto-recharge** when the pool runs low, and **self-serve teams beyond 50 seats**.

Is Team pricing per-seat? Can I add a freelancer just for a project? 

No. Team pricing is based on **your monthly credit commit** , not per-seat. Invite a freelancer, they draw from the shared credit pool while they're working, then remove them when the project wraps. **No charge for adding or removing people.** Member count is capped at **50** today; if you hit the cap, [ contact support ](https://portal.usepylon.com/comfy-org/forms/question) for additional seats.

What carries over when I upgrade my workspace to a Team plan? 

Everything stays. You're upgrading the workspace itself, so **workflows, models, run history, and top-up credits all remain attached**. The only exception: unused credits from your old plan expire at the end of your current billing cycle, since you're moving to a new credit allowance. **Top-up credits carry over.**

What do the Team plan tiers cost, and how does the discount work? 

Team plans come in **five tiers from $200 to $2,500/month** , set by a credit-commit slider. A bigger monthly commit means a bigger discount: **annual plans go up to 20% off, monthly plans up to 10%**. The discount starts at the $400 tier (5% annual / 2.5% monthly) and scales from there.

What collaboration features are included at launch? 

At launch, a Team plan gives you **shared infrastructure** : one credit pool, one bill, one set of admins. **Workflow and asset sharing inside the workspace is coming soon.** In the meantime, to hand off, share the workflow, export the workflow JSON, or drop a Comfy-generated asset into another canvas.

How does concurrency work on a Team plan? Can multiple members run workflows at the same time? 

Yes. On a Team plan, the workspace has a greater concurrency limit per member than the Pro plan. If you run into issues you can request additional support for your team plan limits [ here ](https://comfysupport.portal.usepylon.com/forms/team-plan-requests).

What are the runtime and concurrency limits? 

Each workflow has a max runtime of **30 minutes** on Standard and Creator, raised to **1 hour** on Pro. Jobs over the limit are cancelled automatically to keep the system fair and stable. You can queue up to **100 workflows** at once, and run **1 / 3 / 5** concurrently via API on Standard / Creator / Pro. If you need to increase your Team plan concurrency limit, seats or API rate limits, contact us [ here ](https://comfysupport.portal.usepylon.com/forms/team-plan-requests).

Can I run workflows via API? 

Yes. Run Comfy workflows programmatically via API. Concurrency limits scale with your plan: 1 / 3 / 5 on Standard / Creator / Pro. It's built for integrating ComfyUI into your apps, automating batch jobs, or running production pipelines. If you need to request increasing limits you can do so [ here ](https://comfysupport.portal.usepylon.com/forms/team-plan-requests).

How do I get started with the Cloud API? 

The Cloud API lets you run your ComfyUI workflows on Comfy Cloud from your own apps, scripts, and pipelines, billed from the same credit pool as your plan. Every paid plan includes API access, with concurrency limits of **1 / 3 / 5 / 25** workflows on Standard / Creator / Pro / Team. See the [ Cloud API documentation ](https://docs.comfy.org/development/deploy/cloud) for setup, authentication, and examples.

What are Partner Nodes, and do they cost extra? 

Partner Nodes let you run proprietary models (like Nano Banana Pro) directly inside your workflow. They draw from the same credit pool as your subscription (no separate bill); how much each call costs depends on the model and parameters you set. These credits work across both Comfy Cloud and Comfy Desktop. [ Read more about Partner Nodes ](https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/partner-nodes/overview).

Does Comfy support BYOK (bring your own key)? Can I bring my own provider key? 

Yes. We support BYOK for **Runway and Google Gemini** today, and we can add other providers on request for Enterprise customers. BYOK is turned on per workspace, and Partner Node calls that run on your own key are billed by that provider directly instead of drawing your Comfy credits. [ Submit an Enterprise request ](https://www.comfy.org/contact/) and we'll get you set up.

Can I change or cancel my plan? Do you offer refunds? 

You can cancel any time. Cancelling stops all future payments immediately, and your plan stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for. For refunds, submit a support ticket. These are reviewed by our team case by case.

Where can I find my invoices or add my company's tax ID? 

You can manage all billing details directly through your Stripe portal. Go to Settings → Plans & Credits → Invoice History to open it. From there, you can view and download invoices, update your billing information, and add your company's tax ID.

What if I'm running Comfy at scale? 

For teams running Comfy in production and at scale, Enterprise adds higher API rate limits, advanced security, and dedicated support. [ Learn more about Enterprise ](https://www.comfy.org/enterprise/) or reach out at [ enterprise@comfy.org ](mailto:enterprise@comfy.org).

Do you offer professional services or hands-on creative support? 

Yes, on Enterprise. Forward Deployed Creatives are Comfy experts who embed with your team, build production workflows in your environment, and train your team to run, modify, and extend them. What they build is yours. It's available as an add-on to a Comfy Enterprise agreement. [ Meet the Forward Deployed Creatives ](https://www.comfy.org/forward-deployed-creatives/) or [ contact us ](https://www.comfy.org/contact/).

Do you offer commercial licenses for open-weight models like MiniMax H3? 

Yes. Comfy is the only official reseller of MiniMax commercial-use licenses, covering MiniMax H3 plus MiniMax Audio & Music for running the models locally on your own hardware. Licenses come in two tiers, Professional and Enterprise. See the [ MiniMax Commercial License page ](https://www.comfy.org/minimax/license/), or [ contact sales ](https://www.comfy.org/contact/) to request one.

How many Comfy Agent tasks can I run at the same time? 

Each person in a workspace can run a set number of Comfy Agent tasks at once, on top of the workspace-wide limit for your plan: **2** per person on the Free plan, and **4** per person on Standard, Creator, Pro, Team and Enterprise. If you start more than that, the extra tasks wait in a queue and start automatically as earlier ones finish, so none of your conversations are cut off.
