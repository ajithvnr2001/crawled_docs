## Precise, never cute.

Direct
    We state things. We don’t hedge, qualify, or suggest. Short sentences. Active voice. One idea at a time.

Precise
    We use the real names for things. Nodes, samplers, seeds, checkpoints. We don’t talk around the product or reach for metaphor when the technical term is already good.

Human-first
    The human creates. Comfy makes every step visible. We never write as though the AI is doing the work.

Anti-hype
    We don’t write “stunning,” “revolutionary,” or “effortless.” We don’t promise magic. Our tagline says exactly what we mean: Method, not magic.
