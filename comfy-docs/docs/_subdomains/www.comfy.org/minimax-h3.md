## Q&A

### What is MiniMax H3?

MiniMax's video model, available as Open Weights and through Partner Nodes. It takes text, image, or audio in and renders a clip with native stereo audio. On Comfy you direct it on the canvas alongside every other model. Read [the day-0 launch post](https://blog.comfy.org/p/minimax-h3-day-0-support-in-comfyui) for the technical details.

### How much does MiniMax H3 cost per generation?

It runs on pay-as-you-go or subscription credits. You can draft the same shot free on Wan 2.2 first, and spend credits only on the final render.

### What resolution and clip length does MiniMax H3 support?

Up to 2K, and 5 to 15 seconds per generation.

### Does MiniMax H3 generate audio?

Yes. Every clip carries native stereo audio. When you feed it input audio, H3 conditions on that track instead of overwriting or dropping it.

### Is MiniMax H3 available as Open Weights?

Yes. H3 is available as Open Weights, so you can run it yourself, and through Partner Nodes on Comfy Cloud. To use what you make locally for commercial work, [get an H3 commercial license through Comfy](https://comfy.org/minimax/license/).

### How do I run MiniMax H3 in ComfyUI?

Open a MiniMax workflow template, or add H3 to any workflow on the canvas. It runs on Comfy Cloud, so you do not need a local GPU. Follow [the MiniMax H3 workflow tutorial](https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/video/minimax/minimax-h3) for a step-by-step walkthrough.
