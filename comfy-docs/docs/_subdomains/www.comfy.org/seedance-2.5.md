## Q&A

### What is Seedance 2.5?

ByteDance's cinematic video model. Give it a text prompt or a reference image and it renders the shot with native audio. On Comfy you direct it on the canvas alongside every other model.

### What's new in Seedance 2.5 vs Seedance 2.0?

Longer native clips and support for many more reference inputs than 2.0.

### How do I run Seedance 2.5 in ComfyUI?

Open a Seedance workflow template, or add Seedance to any workflow on the canvas. It runs on Comfy Cloud, so you do not need a local GPU.

### How long can Seedance 2.5 videos be?

Up to 30 seconds natively, and you can chain shots on the canvas when you need a longer cut.

### Does Seedance 2.5 generate audio?

Yes. Dialogue, music and sound effects are generated with the frame, so you do not have to score the clip afterwards.

### Is Seedance 2.5 free to use?

Seedance runs on pay-as-you-go or subscription credits. You can draft the same shot free on Wan 2.2 first, and spend credits only on the final render.

### Can I use the videos commercially?

Yes. Renders include commercial use and carry no watermark.
