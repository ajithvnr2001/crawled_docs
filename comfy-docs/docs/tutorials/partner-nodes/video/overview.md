Choose the right video model for your use case: text-to-video, image-to-video, video enhancement, and more

Copy pageCopy page

ComfyUI Partner Nodes give you access to hosted video models from providers such as Black Forest Labs, ByteDance, Google, Kling, Luma, Runway, and Topaz, so you can generate video without a local GPU. This page helps you pick the right model for text-to-video, image-to-video, video enhancement, or motion control.

## 

​

What you can do with video models

  * **Text-to-video** : Generate a video from a text prompt
  * **Image-to-video** : Animate a still image to bring a photo or artwork to life
  * **Video enhancement** : Upscale, interpolate frames, or improve video quality
  * **Motion control** : Control camera movement, subject motion, or character motion in generated videos



## 

​

Choosing a model by task

### 

​

General-purpose text-to-video

  * **Seedance 2.5 / 2.0** (ByteDance): strong all-round text-to-video generation
  * **FLUX 3 Video** (Black Forest Labs): Black Forest Labs’ video model, built on the FLUX family
  * **Wan 3.0 / Wan 2.7** (Wan): open-model video generation with good motion quality
  * **Grok Imagine Video 1.5** (Grok): fast text-to-video from xAI



### 

​

Image-to-video

  * **Luma Image-to-Video** (Luma): turn a single image into a short animated clip
  * **Kling 3.0** (Kling): image-to-video and text-to-video with strong motion
  * **Runway Video Generation** (Runway): image-to-video and text-to-video with cinematic quality
  * **HappyHorse 1.1 / 1.0** (HappyHorse): image-driven animation



### 

​

Video enhancement & upscaling

  * **FLUX Video Upscale** (Black Forest Labs): upscale and enhance generated video
  * **Topaz Video Enhance Starlight Precise 2.5** (Topaz): upscaling and enhancement
  * **Topaz Astra / Video Enhance Astra** (Topaz): quality restoration tools



### 

​

Motion control & character animation

  * **Kling Motion Control** (Kling): control camera and subject motion
  * **Seedance 2.0 Real Human** (ByteDance): human motion and character animation
