# Plan & Credits

Copy pageCopy page

Open Plan & Credits in Settings to check your balance, top up for Partner Nodes, review usage activity, and (on Cloud) manage invoices.

Copy pageCopy page

The settings described on this page are located in the **ComfyUI in-app settings menu**. You can open it by navigating to **Settings** (gear icon) or using the `Ctrl + ,` keyboard shortcut, then select the corresponding category to view and configure these options.

Credits power **Partner Nodes** (API calls to closed-source models). ComfyUI stays fully open-source and free for local graph execution; credits only apply when you run Partner Nodes or use Cloud billing features. After you sign in under **Settings → User** , open **Settings → Workspace → Plan & Credits**. On Comfy Cloud you can also open the same panel from the profile menu with **Manage plan**. ![Plan & Credits in Settings \(Cloud\)](https://mintcdn.com/dripart/svxMC8DHZXobTT5E/images/interface/setting/plan-credits-cloud-overview.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=svxMC8DHZXobTT5E&q=85&s=48ba78fcb22503d13a7b8a777d78cb4d)

ComfyUI will always remain fully open-source and free for local users. Credit balances in screenshots are example data for illustration.

How to purchase credits

1

Log in to your ComfyUI account

Open **Settings → User** and sign in.![Login Interface](https://mintcdn.com/dripart/kl0baiKht4v1pyjS/images/interface/setting/user.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=kl0baiKht4v1pyjS&q=85&s=4aacfc636460e073d16ef1fdaccb0888)

2

Open Plan & Credits

After login, open **Settings → Workspace → Plan & Credits** (or Cloud profile → **Manage plan**).![Plan & Credits](https://mintcdn.com/dripart/svxMC8DHZXobTT5E/images/interface/setting/plan-credits-cloud-overview.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=svxMC8DHZXobTT5E&q=85&s=48ba78fcb22503d13a7b8a777d78cb4d)

3

Set the amount of credits to purchase

![Set Purchase Amount](https://mintcdn.com/dripart/svxMC8DHZXobTT5E/images/interface/setting/buy.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=svxMC8DHZXobTT5E&q=85&s=471307f74a4e0f6db15b260b9cc2b0b3)In the popup, set the purchase amount and click **Buy**.

4

Confirm and complete payment

![Confirm purchase](https://mintcdn.com/dripart/svxMC8DHZXobTT5E/images/interface/setting/purchase-1.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=svxMC8DHZXobTT5E&q=85&s=d4371030bb5549d08feb37abd8ae19fb)Review the amount, then continue to Stripe checkout to finish payment. Use the same email as your ComfyUI account.

5

Confirm your balance

![Plan & Credits after purchase](https://mintcdn.com/dripart/svxMC8DHZXobTT5E/images/interface/setting/purchase-2.jpg?fit=max&auto=format&n=svxMC8DHZXobTT5E&q=85&s=813309247194d567b0f6df0cd424e7f8)Return to **Plan & Credits** and check that the balance updated. Refresh or restart if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can credits go negative?

Credits are not intended as a credit line. Partner Nodes sometimes report cost after execution, so one run can leave a temporary negative balance. While the balance is negative, Partner Nodes stay blocked until you top up.

Can I get a refund for unused credits?

Currently we do not support refunds for unused credits.

How do I check my current balance and usage?

Open **Settings → Workspace → Plan & Credits**. Use the **Credits** tab for balance and the **Activity** tab for usage history.

Can I share my credits with other users?

You can sign into the same account on multiple devices. Sharing credits with other user accounts is not supported. On Cloud Team workspaces, members share the workspace balance under owner-set limits.

How can I know how much the node will cost?

Check the price badge on the API / Partner Node before running. Actual cost can vary with image size, generation count, and other parameters.

Why don't I see WeChat or Alipay payment options?

Choose payment in Comfy Credits. WeChat and Alipay are only available for that currency path.

Do credits expire?

Yes, expiration depends on the type:

  * **Monthly credits** : Expire at the end of your billing period
  * **Top-up credits** : Expire 1 year from the purchase date
