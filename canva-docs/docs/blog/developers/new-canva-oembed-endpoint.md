# Canva's new oEmbed endpoint

Canva is changing the url of its oEmbed endpoint

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/thumbnail.468d19cb.jpg)

* * *

  * 


Farnaz TavakolJun 1, 2024

* * *

We are in the process of updating our oEmbed endpoint. The current endpoint, `https://www.canva.com/_oembed`, will be eventually replaced with our new endpoint, `https://api.canva.com/_spi/presentation/_oembed`.

We are going to keep the current endpoint operational until 30 Jun 2024.
