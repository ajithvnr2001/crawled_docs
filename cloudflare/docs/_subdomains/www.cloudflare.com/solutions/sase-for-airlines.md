### Frequently asked questions

###### What is Cloudflare One, and how is it different from other solutions on the market?

Cloudflare One is a unified SASE platform built on a composable, programmable global network, not stitched together from acquisitions like first-generation providers. It secures crews, third parties, and passengers using one client and one control plane. Airlines never double-pay for seats and bandwidth.

###### How does Cloudflare secure in-flight Wi-Fi on LEO satellite networks like Starlink?

LEO satellite networks offer incredible speed but lack native content filtering and use dynamic IPs. Cloudflare solves this using Gateway DNS filtering with dedicated resolver IPs. This clientless approach successfully isolates passenger and crew traffic without requiring custom certificates on passenger devices.

###### Why should airlines replace legacy VPNs with Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA)?

Legacy VPNs grant broad network access to pilots logging in from dozens of countries, making dangerous lateral movement trivial once compromised. Replacing these obsolete tools with ZTNA ensures every connection is verified by identity and context, eliminating lateral movement risks and securing nomadic aviation workforces.

###### How does a SASE platform help airlines manage shadow AI and protect passenger data (PNR)?

Airline AI adoption has surged, increasing the risk of exposing Passenger Name Records (PNR). Cloudflare One helps admins enforce policies separating approved and unapproved AI tools. It protects sensitive data by analyzing AI prompt content and intent plus securely logging every single prompt for total visibility.

###### How does Cloudflare help protect against phishing and business email compromise (BEC)?

Airline domains are high-value targets for impersonation, while daily communications from ground handlers and alliance partners increase supply-chain vulnerabilities. Cloudflare’s email security stops highly targeted phishing and BEC before mailbox delivery and tightens DMARC enforcement to protect your airline’s brand identity.

###### What is coffee shop networking and why does it benefit the airlines industry?

Airlines operate incredibly distributed physical footprints, including airports, call centers, and MRO hangars. "Coffee shop networking" replaces expensive, rigid MPLS lines and heavy branch firewalls with cloud-delivered SASE. It treats every airline location like a public Wi-Fi network, securing traffic natively from the cloud.
