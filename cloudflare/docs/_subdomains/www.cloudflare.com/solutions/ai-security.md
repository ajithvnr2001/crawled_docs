### Frequently asked questions

###### What are the top risks of AI security?

Top AI risks include data poisoning, prompt injection, model abuse, intellectual property theft, and sensitive data exposure through AI model outputs.

###### How does Cloudflare secure AI?

Cloudflare secures AI with a unified platform that protects both internal workforce tools and public applications. It helps you discover unapproved AI usage (shadow AI), shield your models from abuse, secure how AI agents access data, and prevent sensitive information from being exposed in prompts.

###### What is AI security posture management (AI-SPM)?

AI-SPM constantly manages your AI security by finding and fixing model vulnerabilities. We simplify this with CASB integrations for tools like ChatGPT and Gemini that automatically surface misconfigurations and other security risks.

###### How do AI Gateway and Cloudflare One work together?

AI Gateway secures and manages model traffic, giving you visibility, controls, and guardrails for every request to and from AI providers. Cloudflare’s SASE platform, Cloudflare One, extends that protection across users, devices, and applications so AI usage stays controlled end to end.

###### What are the benefits of using Cloudflare for AI security?

Cloudflare provides unified security for public and private AI on a single platform. It protects the entire AI lifecycle with a future-proof global network, AI-powered threat detection, and model-agnostic controls, while also offering a platform that empowers developers to build AI apps securely.

###### Can you provide an example of how Cloudflare AI Security Suite works in a real-world scenario?

A remote employee tries to use an unsanctioned AI app to summarize a confidential document and includes prompts that suggest sensitive customer data may be leaving the company. Cloudflare’s SASE platform detects the shadow AI app, analyzes the prompt content and intent, and uses AI security controls to block or steer the request before sensitive data is exposed.
