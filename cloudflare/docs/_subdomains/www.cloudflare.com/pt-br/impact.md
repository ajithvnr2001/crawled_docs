### More accessible

Cloudflare is making the Internet more accessible by improving reliability around the world.

  * Learn more about free Cloudflare [services](https://www.cloudflare.com/pt-br/plans/) that are protecting and improving the performance of millions of web properties.
  * See how the Cloudflare network is expanding digital access [globally](https://blog.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-connected-in-over-300-cities/) by delivering Internet content more efficiently.
  * Learn how Cloudflare is helping make routing Internet traffic more reliable by eliminating [hijacked routes](https://blog.cloudflare.com/is-bgp-safe-yet-rpki-routing-security-initiative/).



![Remote work illustration](https://www.cloudflare.com/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto/https://cf-assets.www.cloudflare.com/v2/image/e44ht8l9dt2kvf9mc8eidc2n03/26Q2_SPOT-ILL_remote-work-illustration_en-US.png)

### More private

Cloudflare is engineering privacy protections into the basic infrastructure of the Internet.

  * Cloudflare is working with [leading technology companies](https://www.cloudflare.com/pt-br/partners/technology-partners/) to secure web browsing by encrypting [DNS](https://blog.cloudflare.com/announcing-1111/) and improving [TLS](https://blog.cloudflare.com/encrypted-client-hello/), as well as building privacy-oriented web services like [privacy-first analytics](https://blog.cloudflare.com/free-privacy-first-analytics-for-a-better-web/).
  * Cloudflare demonstrates our respect for privacy with [public commitments, reporting](https://www.cloudflare.com/pt-br/transparency/), and [audits](https://blog.cloudflare.com/announcing-the-results-of-the-1-1-1-1-public-dns-resolver-privacy-examination/).
  * We are committed to providing built-in [data protection and privacy tools](https://www.cloudflare.com/pt-br/trust-hub/) for every product in our portfolio.
  * Cloudflare is building [quantum-resistant cryptography](https://blog.cloudflare.com/securing-the-post-quantum-world/) to help secure the future of privacy.



![WAF sport illustration](https://www.cloudflare.com/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto/https://cf-assets.www.cloudflare.com/v2/image/oh53oa2eoh1vr42ucis6nri57p/26Q2_SPOT-ILL_waf-sport-illustration_en-US.png)

### More secure

Our network is designed to help band businesses, people, and organizations together to be stronger than malicious actors online.

  * Learn about our free [unmetered DDoS mitigation](https://blog.cloudflare.com/unmetered-mitigation/) and [universal SSL encryption](https://blog.cloudflare.com/introducing-universal-ssl/).
  * Learn how Cloudflare is protecting [civil rights and social justice organizations](https://blog.cloudflare.com/cyberattacks-since-the-murder-of-george-floyd/) as well as [democratic elections](https://www.cloudflare.com/pt-br/election-security/) online.
  * Cloudflare provides free services to protect vulnerable [nonprofits, journalists, independent media voices](https://www.cloudflare.com/pt-br/galileo/), [democratic elections](https://www.cloudflare.com/pt-br/athenian/), and [political campaigns](https://www.cloudflare.com/pt-br/campaigns/) from cyber attacks.



![Request of home illustration](https://www.cloudflare.com/cdn-cgi/image/format=auto/https://cf-assets.www.cloudflare.com/v2/image/84iatfkbjh1kj8dikcfgspcc6g/26Q2_SPOT-ILL_request-of-home-illustration_en-US.png)

### Building together

Cloudflare is part of a community that is striving to make the Internet a force for good.

  * Learn how Cloudflare works with civil society to monitor trends, threats, and Internet shutdowns with [Cloudflare Radar](https://radar.cloudflare.com/).
  * Cloudflare works with the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC), Interpol, and nearly 60 other public and [private agencies](https://www.inhope.org/EN), and has developed [free tools](https://blog.cloudflare.com/the-csam-scanning-tool/) to fight child sexual abuse material online. Learn about our guiding principles in [handling abuse reports](https://www.cloudflare.com/pt-br/trust-hub/abuse-approach/).
  * We collaborate with partner organizations to develop new Internet [standards and protocols](https://blog.cloudflare.com/tag/ietf/) and address [Internet policy and governance](https://www.internetjurisdiction.net/) challenges.

## Athenian Project

**Free security and performance for state and local election websites.**
