### Get early access: Cloudflare pay per crawl beta
Welcome to the private beta of Cloudflare's revolutionary [pay per crawl](http://blog.cloudflare.com/introducing-pay-per-crawl/) feature. Join us in shaping the future of the web where creators are compensated for their work and AI companies can easily access high-quality content.
For publishers and content creators
For crawlers and AI companies
  * Stop scraping: Choose upfront to explicitly allow or deny AI bots from crawling your content.
  * Control access: Decide who can access your content and under what conditions.
  * New revenue stream: Generate income by charging companies for access to your content.


### Frequently asked questions
Why is Cloudflare introducing pay per crawl?
Cloudflare is introducing a new technical solution with pay per crawl to experiment with a way to help content creators be compensated for their contributions to the AI economy. Pay per crawl will let creators control access and get paid, ensuring AI companies can use quality content the right way — with permission and compensation. Over time, we expect this to continue to evolve and may incorporate a combination of different tools for publishers and creators.
How will publishers use pay per crawl?
With pay per crawl, publishers can block AI crawlers, allow specific ones, charge for access, or grant free access. They have full control over how their content is accessed.
How will AI companies use pay per crawl to access content?
AI crawler owners will be able to use pay per crawl to register, see pricing, and choose to pay or walk away — simple, transparent access to quality content.
How will AI bots be identified and distinguished from search engine crawlers or other legitimate bots?
Cloudflare powers one of the world’s largest networks, helping to manage and protect traffic for 20% of the web. Cloudflare handles trillions of requests daily, and thus has the world’s most advanced bot management solutions, accurately distinguishing between human users and AI crawlers. Cloudflare identifies and distinguishes AI crawlers through its sophisticated bot detection system, which combines machine learning, behavioral analysis, and fingerprinting. These tools accurately classify traffic and separate AI crawlers from search engine bots and other “good” bots. Cloudflare also runs one of the world’s largest [Verified Bots programs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/bots/concepts/bot/verified-bots/), which allows it to detect AI crawlers with high precision and for bot operators to self declare the purpose or intent of their crawlers.
What is the difference between AI Crawl Control vs pay per crawl?
AI Crawl Control lets users view and control crawler behavior on their sites. It is available now on all plans. Pay per crawl is a private beta feature of AI Crawl Control that allows publishers to charge for crawler access.
What happens if I join the pay per crawl private beta?
As a beta tester, you will get early access to pay per crawl features, allowing you to experience and provide feedback on pay per crawl. You will have the opportunity to shape the development and future of content access and monetization.
How do I participate in the private beta?
Apply for private beta access by filling out the form at the bottom of the page. Make sure to specify whether you're a publisher or a crawler.
