## One network — everywhere
![](https://cf-assets.www.cloudflare.com/slt3lc6tev37/2xYY4ufl4hchGZRTWg944r/48ace39afb25bdcb2b878c8be5bb8df0/one-network-everywhere-spot-illustration.svg)
The Cloudflare global network runs every service in every data center so your users have a consistent experience everywhere — whether they are in Chicago or Cape Town. This means all customer traffic is processed at the data center closest to its source, with no backhauling or performance tradeoffs. And with single-pass inspection, security is streamlined and built-in.
![](https://cf-assets.www.cloudflare.com/slt3lc6tev37/2xYY4ufl4hchGZRTWg944r/48ace39afb25bdcb2b878c8be5bb8df0/one-network-everywhere-spot-illustration.svg)

##### Security without tradeoffs
We do not just secure the network — we secure the whole company. And we use our own security products. We ensure [TLS](https://www.cloudflare.com/en-gb/learning/ssl/transport-layer-security-tls/) encryption for all data in transit and maintain strict physical security standards. We lead the industry in enforcing security hygiene and protecting the fundamental layers of the Internet such as [DNS](https://www.cloudflare.com/en-gb/learning/dns/dns-over-tls/) and [BGP](https://www.cloudflare.com/en-gb/learning/security/glossary/bgp-hijacking/).
[Learn more](https://blog.cloudflare.com/dogfooding-from-home/)

##### Privacy first
Cloudflare builds data privacy into everything we do. Since we do not generate revenue from advertising, we default against the collection and retention of personal data we process on your behalf. We have committed to challenging government requests for access to encryption and authentication keys.
