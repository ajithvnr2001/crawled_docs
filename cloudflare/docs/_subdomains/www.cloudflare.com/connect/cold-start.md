## Frequently asked questions

Judging criteria, eligibility, and competition terms.

Who is eligible to enter?

The competition is open to companies that:

  * Are based in the United States or Canada
  * Have raised less than USD $10 million in total funding
  * Have a real product or working prototype
  * Are not in stealth mode, waitlist-only, or at the idea-only stage
  * All entrants are 18 years of age or older
  * Do not involve weapons, sexual content, political content, illegal activity, or other categories Cloudflare determines are inappropriate



What are the judging criteria?

Entrants will be judged on product quality (20%), market opportunity and traction (20%), team and pitch strength (20%), the range of Cloudflare products and services used (20%), and the variety of industries represented (20%).
