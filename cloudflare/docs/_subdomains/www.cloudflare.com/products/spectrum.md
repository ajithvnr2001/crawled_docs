Reduce latency for custom applications 

Spectrum helps provide fast, reliable performance for apps that depend on low latency — from online games to financial services. With Argo Smart Routing, TCP traffic is sent faster than the ‘best-effort’ Internet, ensuring a fast and consistent end-user experience. This results in increased user engagement and satisfaction.

DDoS Attack Protection 

Spectrum shields your applications from complex, large-scale attacks with unmetered DDoS protection. Built on a network of 330 locations worldwide, it mitigates even the largest DDoS attacks — before they reach your server. This ensures that your applications remain available and secure.

Granular IP Firewall Control 

Allow or deny individual IPs or IP ranges to restrict which traffic gets sent to your application servers. Spectrum's built-in, software-defined IP firewall makes it easy to control the flow of traffic without hardware or costly maintenance. This results in increased security and control over your application's traffic.

Automatic Failover and Uptime 

With Load Balancing, all active TCP connections and UDP traffic automatically failover to a healthy server, helping increase the uptime of your services. As a result, you can minimize downtime and ensure high availability.
