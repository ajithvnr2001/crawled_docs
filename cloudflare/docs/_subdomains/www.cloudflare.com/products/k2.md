## Serverless, durable event streams

### K2 enables applications to produce events to a durable, ordered stream. Subscribe to the stream with one or more consumers.

[ Start building for free ](https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up) [ View docs ](https://developers.cloudflare.com/k2/)

**Truly serverless**

No clusters to manage or scale. Pay only for what you use.

**Long-term retention**

Events are stored with configurable TTL, up to one month.

**Flexible consumption**

Consume all events from multiple clients, distribute events across a cluster of clients, or both.

### Produce from anywhere. Consume at your own pace.

Append records from a Worker or over HTTP, then read them through subscriptions that track their own position in the stream.

Produce records from a Worker 

Add a `k2` binding to your Wrangler config and call `send()` with a batch of records. Batch writes are atomic, and the binding handles authentication for you.

Consume through a subscription 

Lease a batch of records, process it, then `ack` to advance the subscription or `nack` to have the records delivered again.

### How K2 works

![Background Pattern](https://www.cloudflare.com/static/pattern.png)

### Create a stream

Create a stream from the dashboard, Wrangler, or the REST API, and set the retention period.

![Background Pattern](https://www.cloudflare.com/static/pattern.png)

### Produce records

Append batches of records from a Workers binding or over HTTP. Once a batch is accepted, it is stored durably.

![Background Pattern](https://www.cloudflare.com/static/pattern.png)

### Subscribe

Create a subscription to track progress. Share one subscription across many consumers to split the work, or create several to fan out.

![Background Pattern](https://www.cloudflare.com/static/pattern.png)

### Consume and acknowledge

Consumers lease a batch of records and acknowledge it once processed. Records that fail processing, or whose lease expires, are delivered again, giving you at-least-once delivery.

![Background Pattern](https://www.cloudflare.com/static/pattern.png)
