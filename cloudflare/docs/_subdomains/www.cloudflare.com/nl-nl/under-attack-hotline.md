##### Step 1. Onboarding and diagnosis
A Cloudflare security specialist will reach out, gather the necessary information to get started, and diagnose the type of attack that is occurring.

Our Cloudflare attack mitigation team will work with you to scope, mitigate, and match you with the appropriate solutions to stop the attack.
![](https://cf-assets.www.cloudflare.com/slt3lc6tev37/2iaZGHR7NSRdBzpq8wiboE/de2d9d0f908ec37deea865b055af42df/cloudflare-spectrum.png)
##### Step 3. Remediation and monitoring
Once onboarded, users will be able to monitor the impact of the attack and affected properties, and begin remediation planning with a Cloudflare specialist.
