# Project Fair Shot
![Illustration of vaccine shot and vial](https://cf-assets.www.cloudflare.com/brandfolder/project-fair-shot.svg)
Around the world, governments, hospitals, and pharmacies are struggling to distribute the COVID-19 vaccine. Technical limitations are causing vaccine registration sites to crash under the load of registrations.
At Cloudflare, we want to help.
![Illustration of vaccine shot and vial](https://cf-assets.www.cloudflare.com/brandfolder/project-fair-shot.svg)
## Cloudflare's Waiting Room
![Cloudflare waiting room spot illustration](https://cf-assets.www.cloudflare.com/slt3lc6tev37/6aIAcylAmcVdLv8SVp2i9X/befc5471b4e7bcf9b05868376c752bef/cloudflare-waiting-room-spot-illustration.svg)
Cloudflare's Waiting Room feature allows organizations with more demand for a resource — be it concert tickets, new edition sneakers, or vaccines — to allow individuals to queue and then allocate access. Waiting Rooms can be deployed in front of any existing registration website without requiring code changes.
In 2022, Project Fair Shot received a [Webby Award](https://winners.webbyawards.com/2022/apps-and-software/software-services-platforms/event-management/220961/cloudflares-project-fair-shot) in the category of People's Voice Winner.
![Cloudflare waiting room spot illustration](https://cf-assets.www.cloudflare.com/slt3lc6tev37/6aIAcylAmcVdLv8SVp2i9X/befc5471b4e7bcf9b05868376c752bef/cloudflare-waiting-room-spot-illustration.svg)
### As we watched the world struggle to fairly and efficiently distribute the COVID-19 vaccine, we wanted to lend our technologies and expertise to help. Under Project Fair Shot, Cloudflare is providing Waiting Room to any government agency, hospital, pharmacy, or other organization facilitating the distribution of the COVID-19 vaccine for free.
