## TLD Rankings by DNS Magnitude

Copy link

Top-level domains ranked by their **DNS Magnitude** — a metric estimating the popularity of a TLD based on the number of **unique networks** querying Cloudflare’s 1.1.1.1 resolver.  
  
The magnitude value ranges from 0 to 10, with higher values (closer to 10) indicating that the TLD is queried by a broader range of clients, reflecting greater global visibility and broader usage across the Internet.
