At this time, my colleague came back from his call, and with his help we determined some other things:

## So what does Jest have to do with Steam Locomotives?

[_Nothing, that's what_](https://github.com/jestjs/jest/issues/14046). The whole affair is an unfortunate naming clash between `sl` the Steam Locomotive and `sl` the[ _Sapling CLI_](https://github.com/facebook/sapling?tab=readme-ov-file#sapling-cli). Jest wanted `sl` the source control system, but ended up getting steam-rolled by `sl `the Steam Locomotive.
