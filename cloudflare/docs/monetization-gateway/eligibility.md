# Eligibility

Last updated Sep 30, 2026|Copy as Markdown|[View as Markdown](https://developers.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/eligibility/index.md)|[Agent setup](https://developers.cloudflare.com/agent-setup/)

OverviewMeet seller requirementsMeet zone requirements

Your account and zone must pass eligibility checks. Buyers and sellers must be based in the United States.

## Meet seller requirements

You must meet these seller requirements:

  * Accept the [Service-Specific Terms ↗︎](https://www.cloudflare.com/service-specific-terms-application-services/).
  * Be based in the United States.
  * Keep a credit card on file.
  * Verify your account email address.
  * Use an account older than 60 days.
  * Pass Cloudflare account-level security checks.



## Meet zone requirements

Your zone must meet these requirements:

  * Proxy the zone through Cloudflare.
  * Use a zone older than 30 days.
  * Pass Cloudflare zone-level security checks.
