# Anthropomorphisms

Last updated Aug 20, 2026|Copy as Markdown|[View as Markdown](https://developers.cloudflare.com/style-guide/style-and-grammar/grammar/parts-of-speech/anthropomorphisms/index.md)|[Agent setup](https://developers.cloudflare.com/agent-setup/)

Avoid Anthropomorphisms in product content. Anthropomorphism is attributing human characteristics to inanimate objects. Computers do not think, want, worry, or do other things that are uniquely human.
