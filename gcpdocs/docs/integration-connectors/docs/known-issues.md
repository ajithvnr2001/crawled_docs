### The custom connector page doesn't work as intended  
| Status   |  [**OPEN**](https://docs.cloud.google.com/integration-connectors/docs/release-notes#February_04_2024)  |  
| --- | --- |  
| Issue   |  When you delete a custom connector from the [custom connectors page](https://console.cloud.google.com/connectors/custom-connectors), the page may not work properly until the connector deletion is complete. You will notice this issue only if you stay on the custom connectors page after you click delete. You can navigate to other pages in the Google Cloud console after clicking delete, and this won't interrupt the custom connector deletion process.   |  
| Workaround   | There is no specific workaround for this issue. For the [custom connectors page](https://console.cloud.google.com/connectors/custom-connectors) to work properly, you must wait until the deletion is complete.   |
