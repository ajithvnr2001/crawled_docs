Backup and DR offers a managed solution for backing up and recovering your workloads in Google Cloud. You can centralize management, monitoring, and reporting for your daily backup operations. This approach helps you maintain business continuity, enhance security, and recover faster from unexpected outages.

To use Backup and DR, you define backup plans in the appliance management console. These plans specify when to back up a resource, where to store the backup, and its retention period. The service supports two types of storage for your backups: backup vaults and self-managed storage. A backup vault is a Google-managed, secured, and isolated resource that provides immutability and indelibility, protecting your data from unauthorized changes or deletions. Conversely, self-managed storage, such as Persistent Disk snapshots or Cloud Storage buckets in your Google Cloud projects, lets you manage your own storage. However, self-managed storage lacks the immutability features of backup vaults and can be deleted by users with the appropriate permissions. You can back up various Google Cloud resources, including Compute Engine instances, Google Cloud VMware Engine VMs, and self-managed Oracle and SQL Server databases.
