Backup and DR provides backup and recovery for workloads running in Google Cloud. As a managed service, it offers centralized management, monitoring, and reporting for your daily backup operations. These capabilities help you streamline data protection, strengthen security, and recover faster to minimize business interruptions. Through policy-based management, you define when to back up resources, where to store the backups, and their retention period. The service supports incremental backups, which can help you achieve a lower recovery point objective (RPO), representing the maximum acceptable data loss after an incident. Incremental backups also lead to a lower total cost of ownership (TCO), the overall cost of owning and operating a system.
