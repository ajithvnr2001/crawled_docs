## Background Audio Skip Sections

[Content Studio](https://docs.sarvam.ai/changelog?filter=Content%20Studio)

You can now silence the original background sound for part of your video. In the dubbing editor, right-click the **Background** lane and choose **Add skip section** , then drag the edges to fit. It saves on its own and applies to every language when you export. Voice-only audio exports are not affected. Each project can have up to 50 skip sections. See [Editing a Dub](https://docs.sarvam.ai/creative-dubbing-edit).
