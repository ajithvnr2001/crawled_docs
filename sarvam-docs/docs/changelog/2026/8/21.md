## Shared Cloned Voices

[Content Studio](https://docs.sarvam.ai/changelog?filter=Content%20Studio)

Cloned voices your teammates create in a workspace now show up for you. Open **Voice Library** , then **My voices** , or go to **Instant Voice Cloning** to find them. Only the person who created a voice, an organization owner or admin, or a workspace admin can delete it. See [Managing Your Voice Library](https://docs.sarvam.ai/creative-voice-library-manage).
