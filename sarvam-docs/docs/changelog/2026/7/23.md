## Upload Limits by Plan

[Content Studio](https://docs.sarvam.ai/changelog?filter=Content%20Studio)

The dubbing upload box now shows the limits that apply to your plan. Starter allows files up to 2 GB and 1 hour, Pro up to 3 GB and 1 hour, and Enterprise up to 4 GB and 4 hours.

## Auto Speaker Count for Enterprise

[Content Studio](https://docs.sarvam.ai/changelog?filter=Content%20Studio)

On the Enterprise plan, you can choose **Auto (detect speakers)** under **Number of speakers** when you [create a dub](https://docs.sarvam.ai/creative-dubbing-create). Content Studio detects the number of speakers for you, so you don’t need to count them before you upload. To get access to the Enterprise plan, [contact us](https://www.sarvam.ai/contact-us).
