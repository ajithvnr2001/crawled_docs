## Higher-Quality Background Audio

[Content Studio](https://docs.sarvam.ai/changelog?filter=Content%20Studio)

New dubs keep the original background music and sound effects at higher quality. This applies to sources up to 30 minutes long; longer sources keep the standard background quality. Every export, including projects you made earlier, now plays at a steady volume. Dubs you made earlier keep their old background.
