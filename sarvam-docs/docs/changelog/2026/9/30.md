## See Subagent Work in One Row

[Cowork](https://docs.sarvam.ai/changelog?filter=Cowork)

When your agent spawns several helpers at once, they now appear as a single row with a pill for each one, plus a panel showing its name, how long it ran, and its latest steps. Titles, timings, and stopped calls stay visible after you reload the page.
