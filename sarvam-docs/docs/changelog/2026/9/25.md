## Dubbing Export Menu

[Content Studio](https://docs.sarvam.ai/changelog?filter=Content%20Studio)

The **Export** menu in the dubbing editor now has **Video** , **Audio** and **Subtitles** tabs, with a download for each file. Audio comes as WAV, with or without background sound and subtitles come as SRT and VTT, in the dubbed or original language. See [Editing a Dub](https://docs.sarvam.ai/creative-dubbing-edit).
